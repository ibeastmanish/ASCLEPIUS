02

NASA OSDR · OSD-569 and OSD-575 DatasetSpaceX Inspiration4 crew: complete blood count and metabolic panel

The app's own "personal normal" rule was run on the four crew members' pre-flight blood tests. The day after landing, all 4 had a red-cell size (MCV) below their own lowest pre-flight value, while all 4 stayed inside the standard 80–100 fL range. 16 of 34 markers have an index of individuality under 0.6.

We found that row C003 L-92 in the blood-count file is shifted by one column. We corrected it in a documented script, kept the original untouched, and reported it to the NASA OSDR team on 13 Nov 2026.

Every body-map card names its hazard. The Reply Clock is built on Distance from Earth; the Crew Pulse cabin rule on Hostile/Closed Environments.

05

NASA HRP · Risks of Human Spaceflight Challenge resource

25 guidance cards link to 9 HRP risks, such as cardiovascular adaptations, immune responses, nutrition, sleep and teamwork. A test checks that every card names a real risk.

Artemis II Standard Measures shaped the fixed six-reading daily check. The Artemis II well-being and sleep study and the Immune Biomarkers study inform Crew Pulse's sleep and infection rules.

08

NASA OCHMO-TB-004 · Carbon Dioxide Technical brief

Headaches reported at 2.8–4.5 mmHg CO2. This is the basis of the Crew Pulse rule "several crew with headaches on the same day: check the cabin air first".
