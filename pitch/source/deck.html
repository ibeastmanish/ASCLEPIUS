02How it works

One minute. Six readings. One clear answer.

Blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, temperature, sleep, mood: the six from the challenge.

Two checks for every reading

The usual adult range and your own learned normal (after 7 checks). The worse result wins.

A body map that explains itself

Every colour shows its rule and its source, and every card names its NASA HRP hazard and risks. No AI makes health decisions.

✓Good!Check✕Act now

Fully offline · encrypted on the device (AES-GCM 256) · Simulated crew data

03New · Earth Reply Clock

Can Earth answer before you must act?

Earth's answer = light time there + the flight surgeon's reply (are they on shift?) + light time back.

✓Earth guides

The answer arrives well before you need to act.

→Act, Earth confirms

Start the steps now; Earth confirms in time.

!Crew decides

The answer comes too late, or the Sun blocks the link.

Example: red result

On 14 Nov 2026 Earth's earliest answer is 27 min if the surgeon is on shift, 52 min if they must be paged. You must act within 10 min. Either way: crew decides.

A Mars day is 39.6 min longer than Earth's, so the crew's morning drifts against Houston's shift. NASA's Earth-Independent Medical Ops concept (NTRS 20250000009) has no delay levels; this gives it numbers.

04New · Crew Pulse

One person, the cabin, or something spreading?

1One person flagged

It is about that person. Follow their card.

3Several crew, same day

Suspect the cabin air. Today: 3 of 4 crew have a headache or short sleep, and cabin CO2 reads 3.4 mmHg, above NASA's 3 mmHg limit.

→Fever moving person to person

Possible infection: Kwame (Sol 208) → Mei (Sol 210). 61% of ISS astronauts shed dormant viruses, mostly without symptoms.

05Proof on real NASA crew data

Population range: 4 of 4 fine.
Personal range: 4 of 4 changed.

NASA OSDR OSD-569 (Inspiration4 blood counts). The day after landing, all 4 crew had a red-cell size (MCV) below their own lowest pre-flight value, yet all 4 stayed inside the 80–100 fL adult range.

We found an error in the NASA file

One row (crew C003, draw L-92) is shifted by a column. I fixed it, documented it, and reported it to NASA's OSDR team.

Honest limits: 4 people, 3-day flight, no in-flight draws; MCV still low months later in 3 crew, so a lab change may be part of it. Consistent with a personal change, not proof of one. Image: NASA.

06Impact

Delay is delay.

The same clock works on Earth. Near Warangal, the nearest doctor can be hours away. Clinic mode swaps light time for travel time and clinic hours, with the daily check in Telugu and Hindi (drafts).

Ground Station

A second page gets the signed, encrypted summary only after the real light time, checks the signature, rejects replays, and signs the reply.
